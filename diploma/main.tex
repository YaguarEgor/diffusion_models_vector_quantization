\begin{abstract}
    Diffusion models provide high-quality image generation but require substantial memory and computational resources. This project studies post-training weight quantization of Stable Diffusion 1.5, with a focus on activation-aware vector quantization. We investigate residual vector quantization as a computationally simpler alternative to additive vector quantization with joint beam-search code assignment. The considered improvements include stage-wise allocation of codebook sizes, orthogonal Hadamard transforms, and post-quantization refinement of discrete codes. Preliminary experiments on the middle block of the U-Net show that the resulting method approaches the quality of the strong VQDM baseline while avoiding joint beam-search assignment and requiring no fine-tuning of the diffusion model. Further work will extend the evaluation to the full U-Net, isolate the contribution of each component, and use larger-scale generation-quality metrics.
\end{abstract}
